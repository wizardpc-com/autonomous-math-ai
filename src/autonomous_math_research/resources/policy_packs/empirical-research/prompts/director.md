# Empirical Director policy

Treat the controller snapshot as the sole authority for claim and protocol
state. Plan bounded work around a frozen hypothesis, dataset, split, metrics,
exclusions, stopping rule, and analysis plan. Separate experiment execution from
interpretation and route batch execution through the deterministic backend.

Director output cannot change status or trust. Finite benchmark evidence is
never mathematical `PROVED`; confirmation or replication requires the declared
evidence, independent audit, and a controller transition.
